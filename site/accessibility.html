Accessibility

Every station is step-free, from the street to the train.

Step-free everywhere

All 222 stations have at least two elevators, and platforms are level with the train floor. No ramps, no gaps.

Elevator status

Every station page lists its elevators. If one is out, a pod takes you to the nearest station with working elevators, free.

Pods on demand

Pods wait at every station, 24 hours a day, for the rest of your trip.

How the river boats run

Each river is a loop of docks, one row on each bank. Three kinds of boat use it:

  • Clockwise loop: along one bank, round the end, back along the other.
  • Counterclockwise loop: the same docks in the other direction.
  • Zigzag: straight across, bank to bank, at every dock.

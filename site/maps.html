Reading the map

  • A ring on a station means you can change lines without leaving the platform area.
  • White capsules join stations a short walk apart.
  • Thin purple lines are Regional Rail. Gold inside purple is High-Speed Rail.
  • Dotted colored lines are ferry routes.

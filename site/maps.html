Rapid Transit system map

System mapAll 222 stations, regional rail, high-speed rail and ferries.

Ways to use it

  • Interactive map: tap any station for departures.
  • Download the PDF: vector, prints sharp at any size.
  • Download the PNG: 600 dpi, about 18.5 by 15.8 inches.

Reading the map

  • A ring on a station means you can change lines without leaving the platform area.
  • White capsules join stations a short walk apart.
  • Thin purple lines are Regional Rail. Gold inside purple is High-Speed Rail.
  • Dotted colored lines are ferry routes.

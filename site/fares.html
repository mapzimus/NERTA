Free rides

FREE No Charlie Points needed on:

  • Silver Line, every stop at the airport
  • Tram 23, Ashmont to Nubian
  • Fast Ferry shuttle, Navy Yard, Langone Ferryport and Long Wharf
  • Zigzag boats across the Charles, the Mystic and Chelsea Creek

Ride free

  • Children 12 and under
  • Students, with a school ID
  • Riders 65 and older
  • Riders with disabilities
  • Everyone, on New Year's Eve and the day of the Marathon

Fare capping

Ride as much as you like. Your Charlie Points stop counting once you reach the price of a day pass, so you never pay more than a day pass in a day: 75 Charlie Points.

How to pay

Tap any card, phone or pod at the gate. Your Charlie Points come out of the same account whichever you tap, and a pod ride to the station counts toward your transfer.

Running low? Turn on auto-reload in My Charlie Points and your balance tops itself up.

About this site

This is a fan project. NERTA is not a real transit agency, and nothing here is real service information.

Just for fun

This site imagines what Boston's transit could be: extended lines, four new ones, a rail tunnel joining North Station and South Station, regional rail, high-speed rail and five ferry routes. It is drawn to look and feel like the real thing, because that's the fun of it.

None of it is real. The departures, delays, ridership numbers, fares and news are made up, generated by the site itself. Don't use this site to plan a real trip.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any real transit agency or government body.

Where a line or stop follows a real proposal or plan, the research notes say so. Others are invented.

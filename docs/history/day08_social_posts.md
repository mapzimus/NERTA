## LinkedIn

Day 8 of the 30 Day Map Challenge is Utopia, so I drew my fantasy version of the T.

It's in the style of the wall posters in every station. The center is The BIGGER Dig, a tunnel joining North Station and South Station at a new Boston Central. Around it are a Yellow ring, a Cyan Line from Charlestown to Southie, the Fairmount corridor as the Indigo Line, and a Harbor Loop ferry that follows a ferry chartered in 1631.

This is a dream map, not a plan. Striped lines nod to real proposals or old lines brought back. Dashed lines are simply what I'd love to see.

I also tested it as a network, checking every pair of stops for backtracking. That found gaps a good-looking map hides, and I closed them with new rail links.

There's no date on it. When would you say this gets finished?

This is a fan map, not a real MBTA map.

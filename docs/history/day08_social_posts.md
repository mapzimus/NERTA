## LinkedIn

Day 8 of the 30 Day Map Challenge is Utopia, so I drew Boston's Rapid Transit, my fantasy version of the T.

It's in the style of the wall posters in every station. The center is The BIGGER Dig, a four-track tunnel joining North Station and South Station at a new Boston Central. Around it are a Yellow ring, a Cyan Line from Assembly to Southie, the Fairmount corridor as the Indigo Line, all 13 commuter rail routes, and ferries on both shores.

This is a dream map, not a plan, but most of it starts from real studies and proposals. Striped lines are real proposals. Dashed subway lines are simply what I'd love to see.

I also priced it. My rough estimate is about $125B in 2026 dollars, about five times the original Big Dig once that's adjusted for inflation.

When would you say this gets finished?

This is a fan map, not affiliated with the MBTA.

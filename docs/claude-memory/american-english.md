Max wants American English: "color" not "colour", "gray", "center", "labeled". This applies to anything on the map (titles, legends, labels, post text) and to replies. In R code, prefer `color =` over `colour =`.

**Why:** the maps are published for an American audience, and UK spellings had been creeping in by default (flagged on Day 4, 2026-10-31).
**How to apply:** check legend and label text for UK spellings before showing a preview. See [[map-layout-preferences]].

# Boston Historic "T" Expansion Track Map: what it means for Day 8

Source: vanshnookenraggen, "Boston Historic T Expansion Track Map", v1.0.4, 2023. A fan compilation of official expansion plans from 1897 on. Marked unofficial and not for alteration or distribution without consent, so this note paraphrases it and the map copies none of its art. Its plan histories were read from the PDF's text layer. It counts as one source until checked against another.

## Plans that back stops on our map

| Our map | What the track map shows |
| --- | --- |
| Red to Lexington Route 128 | 1970s plan: Arlington Heights first, then Route 128. Arlington protests cut it back to Alewife, which opened 1985. |
| Red to Waltham | Named as a possible Alewife extension after the 1947 plan. |
| Orange to Reading | 1945 Coolidge Commission plan. Planned into the 1960s, then Melrose, Wakefield and Reading objected. Provisions remain. |
| Orange to West Roxbury | Part of the postwar Needham and Dedham conversions. |
| Orange to Saugus | No Orange plan shown; the old Saugus Branch right-of-way is now a trail. |
| Blue to Lynn | Coolidge Commission plan, postponed for lack of funds. The old right-of-way is redeveloped, so a line would likely run beside the Newburyport Line. |
| Blue to Winthrop | The narrow-gauge Winthrop branch was abandoned in the Coolidge plan. |
| Red-Blue connector | Dropped Big Dig mitigation. An old Joy St connection once carried non-revenue moves. |
| Green to West Medford | Original Green Line Extension concept, with Medford Hillside. Deferred because of the cost of bridging the Mystic. |
| Green to Needham | 1945 plan paired the Riverside and Needham conversions. |
| Green to Watertown | The A branch, replaced with buses in 1969. Tracks gone by 1996. |
| E to Forest Hills | The Arborway Line, cut back in 1985. Restoration was Big Dig mitigation, then the tracks were removed from 2005. |
| Yellow ring | Urban Ring Phase 3 was a rail line, as Green light rail or an Orange branch. Phase 2 was bus lanes at $2.2 billion in 2008. |
| Boston Central | North-South Link studied with up to three stations, including a Central Station near Aquarium. Agrees with the siting brief. |
| West Station | On the Worcester Line and the Grand Junction, tied to the Beacon Park redevelopment. Completion date given as 2040. |

## Precedents for the ideas we invented

- **North End stop:** a 1904 alternative tunnel alignment ran through the North End with two stations.
- **Cyan along the waterfront:** the Atlantic Ave El linked North and South Stations along the seaport until it came down in the 1930s.
- **Cyan in Southie:** elevated lines through South Boston, Chelsea and Everett were proposed and dropped.

## Plans not on our map

- A Blue Line west through Kenmore to Warren St in Brighton, from the 1920s heavy-rail conversion plan.
- A Red Line branch from Andrew along the Fairmount corridor to Blue Hill Ave.
- Red under Washington St to Talbot Ave, before the Shawmut Branch purchase.
- Green to Woburn along the Lowell Line.
- An Edgeworth station on Orange, dropped from the original plan.
- Orange to Route 128 via Hyde Park, and an Ashmont extension to Granite Ave.

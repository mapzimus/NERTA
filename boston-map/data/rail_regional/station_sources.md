# Regional rail station positions

Where each station on the commuter rail poster really sits, and what backs the spot. The points are in `stations.geojson`, built by `src/rail_regional_stations.py`. **Agree** = two sources under 150 m apart. **One source** = unverified. **Town point only** = approx: a town point set on the old line. The first source is the position used. "Placed with OSM" means a written or mapped description (a corner, a siding) turned into a point with OpenStreetMap's streets and old rail lines.

## Notes

Stations not plain MBTA and OSM agreements, in poster order.

- **Boston Central**: the map's own station, placed by hand under the Dig at Atlantic Ave and State St. OSM puts that corner 30 m away.
- **South Station**: MBTA and OSM are 171 m apart. Wikipedia's point is 36 m from MBTA and 135 m from OSM, so the MBTA point is used.
- **Littleton/Route 495**: MBTA and OSM are 254 m apart. Wikipedia's point is 258 m from MBTA and 4 m from OSM, so the OSM point is used.
- **Hudson** (42.39158, -71.56270). Sanborn 1916, Hudson sheet 3, placed with OSM: Fitchburg Division passenger station on Main St opposite High St (Library of Congress); OSM gives the spot between Main St and the old line. Check: B&M's other Hudson station, on the Central Mass line (Wikidata Q133296526, MACRIS HUD.24; Hudson's stop 1939 to 1958, building still standing), 522 m away.
- **Marlborough** (42.34820, -71.55486). Wikidata Q133501665 (MACRIS MRB.103): Marlboro station, B&M, built 1893, since torn down. Sanborn 1920, Marlborough sheet 4, placed with OSM, 62 m away: B&M passenger station on Lincoln St at Mechanic St (Library of Congress); OSM gives the corner.
- **North Wilmington**: MBTA and OSM are 188 m apart. Wikipedia's point is 3 m from MBTA and 186 m from OSM, so the MBTA point is used.
- **South Salem** (42.50778, -70.89811). City of Salem slides 2025, placed with OSM: the January 28, 2025 community update puts the stop at Ocean Ave West, with side platforms and a Jefferson Ave access option; OSM gives where Ocean Ave West meets the Eastern Route. Wikipedia: South Salem station, 18 m away: article coordinates, address Ocean Ave.
- **Peabody** (42.52660, -70.92717). Sanborn 1920, Peabody sheet 11, placed with OSM: passenger station on Central St between the tracks and Mill St (Library of Congress); OSM gives the two corners. Peabody Historical Society, placed with OSM, 78 m away: station at Peabody Square (William Powers, Peabody's Railroads, 1998; last passenger train May 16, 1958); OSM gives the square where Central St meets Main St.
- **Danversport** (42.55702, -70.93020). Sanborn 1927, Danvers sheet 13, placed with OSM: Danversport R.R. Station where the Creese & Cook tannery siding left the line (Library of Congress); OSM gives the siding switch. Powers (1998) and Wikipedia's Essex Railroad route map list the stop but don't place it; OSM's Danversport neighborhood point is 0.7 km away.
- **Danvers** (42.56579, -70.94125). Commons photos 2015: camera GPS of three June 2015 photos of the depot, averaged. Commons: built as Danvers Plains station on the Essex Railroad, moved in 1923 to serve the Newburyport Branch too; the Friends of the Danvers Rail Trail say it has since been moved to a lot off Cherry St. OSM Danvers Junction node, 121 m away: where the Essex line met the Newburyport Branch.
- **Middleton** (42.59749, -71.01441). OSM town point, on the old line: OSM's Middleton town point, moved onto the Middleton Rail Trail (the old Essex Railroad). Wikipedia's route map and the Middleton Rail Trail's history list the station; nothing found places it.
- **Clifton** (42.48265, -70.87794). Commons photo 2017: camera GPS of a photo of the former station site, granite platform edge in view. Wikipedia: Clifton, Massachusetts, placed with OSM, 1 m away: the site is seen from Clifton Ave and the track bed is now a bike path; OSM gives where the Marblehead Rail Trail crosses Clifton Ave.
- **Marblehead** (42.50238, -70.85640). Marblehead Weekly News, placed with OSM: National Grand Bank bought the old B&M depot site on Pleasant St in 1962 and is at 91 Pleasant St; OSM gives the bank building. Sanborn 1915, Marblehead sheet 10, placed with OSM, 55 m away: passenger station on Pleasant St just north of School St (Library of Congress); OSM gives the corner.
- **Manchester**: MBTA and OSM are 164 m apart. Wikipedia's point is 88 m from MBTA and 75 m from OSM, so the MBTA point is used.
- **West Station**: kept where it was placed by hand (Beacon Park Yard, Allston). Wikipedia's point for MassDOT's planned station is 390 m away, at 42.35478, -71.12156.
- **Wellesley Farms**: MBTA and OSM are 173 m apart. Wikipedia's point is 54 m from MBTA and 134 m from OSM, so the MBTA point is used.
- **North Easton** (42.08915, -71.09772). Wikipedia: North Easton station (MBTA), rev. 1279156129: coordinates in the article's last version before it was cut to a redirect in March 2025; address Roche Brothers Way. 2013 Final EIS figure 3.2-22, placed with OSM, 61 m away: the figure (copy on Commons) shows an 800 ft platform on the old Stoughton Branch below the Roche Bros. store, at the Easton/Stoughton line; OSM gives the old bed's nearest point to the store.
- **Easton Village** (42.06927, -71.10332). Wikipedia: North Easton station: Easton Village station redirects here; the South Coast Rail article puts the stop at H.H. Richardson's depot on Oliver St. OSM way/212962983, 0 m away: North Easton Station building (may share a source with Wikipedia: they are 1 m apart). Commons photo 2017, 36 m away: camera GPS of a June 2017 photo of the depot.
- **Raynham Place** (41.98001, -71.07542). Wikipedia: Raynham Place station: article coordinates, behind the Raynham Park racing center. Commons photo 2017, 65 m away: camera GPS of a June 2017 photo of the proposed site (both sources are Wikimedia, possibly one editor). Check: OSM's old Dighton and Somerset bed, 16 m away.
- **Taunton** (41.90444, -71.08222). Wikipedia: Dean Street station: Taunton station (MBTA) redirects here; the platform would sit north of the 1876 Old Colony depot off Dean St (Route 44). Commons photo 2017, 56 m away: camera GPS of a June 2017 photo of the proposed platform area (both sources are Wikimedia). Check: OSM: the Dean Street Industrial Track meets Dean St, 77 m away.
- **Plymouth** (41.98128, -70.69042). MBTA V3 API place-PB-0356: MBTA still lists the stop after the 2021 closure. OSM node/7005396587, 6 m away: tagged a disused station. Wikipedia: Plymouth station (MBTA), 13 m away: article coordinates.

## Sources

- **MBTA**: MBTA V3 API stops (`data/mbta_stops.gpkg`, fetched November 3, 2026). Plymouth from the API's `/stops/place-PB-0356`, still listed after the 2021 closure.
- **OSM**: OpenStreetMap through Overpass, November 7 and 8, 2026, cached in `osm_stations.json` with the streets and old rail lines used to place described sites. A name match more than 3 km from the MBTA point is ignored.
- **Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons**: article coordinates, item coordinates and photo camera GPS, read November 7 and 8, 2026.
- **Sanborn**: Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps, Library of Congress, copies on Wikimedia Commons: Peabody 1920 sheet 11, Danvers 1927 sheet 13, Marblehead 1915 sheet 10, Hudson 1916 sheet 3, Marlborough 1920 sheet 4.
- **Town and city sources**: City of Salem, South Salem stop community update slides (January 28, 2025); Peabody Historical Society ("Ticket to Ride", and William Powers, *Peabody's Railroads*, 1998); Marblehead Weekly News on the National Grand Bank and the depot site; Friends of the Danvers Rail Trail history page; South Coast Rail 2013 Final EIS figure 3.2-22 (copy on Commons).

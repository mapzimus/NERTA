What backs each new piece of the map. **Two** = two sources. **One** = a single source, or several copying one. **Invented** = no source, our idea. "Brief" is the siting brief Max supplied; "track map" is the historic track map in `research/06-historic-track-map.md`. Neither has been checked against the original plans, so **Two** here means two sources agree, not that the primary documents were read.
